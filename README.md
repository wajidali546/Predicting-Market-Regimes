# Predicting Market Regimes in Indian Equity Markets

## Overview
This project uses machine learning and deep learning to analyse and predict market behaviour across NIFTY50, BANKNIFTY, and NIFTYNEXT50. It examines market direction, downside risk, and volatility across different forecasting horizons and market conditions.

## Research Objectives
- Analyse the unpredictable nature of Indian equity markets.
- Compare machine learning and deep learning models for different prediction tasks.
- Identify important predictors across short-term and long-term forecasting horizons.
- Examine differences in market behaviour across indices and market regimes.

## Key Findings
- **Market Regimes:** Extreme price movements are common, and calm and turbulent periods tend to cluster together.
- **Model Performance:** No single model consistently performs best across all prediction tasks and time horizons.
- **Forecasting Horizons:** Short-term forecasts rely more on price and trend information, while longer-term forecasts benefit more from news, macroeconomic conditions, and sentiment.
- **Prediction Tasks:** Market direction is associated more with trend indicators, downside risk with external information, and volatility with combinations of signals.
- **Index Differences:** NIFTY50, BANKNIFTY, and NIFTYNEXT50 respond differently to predictive signals, whose importance also changes across market regimes.

## Methodology
The study compares multiple machine learning and deep learning models to investigate predictive performance across different indices, forecasting horizons, and market conditions.

## Significance
The findings highlight the importance of adaptive model selection and dynamic predictor relevance when analysing financial markets. They demonstrate why market forecasting should account for the prediction objective, time horizon, index characteristics, and prevailing market regime.
